Model & Standardized binary logistic regression, five features, JSON serialized; trained in NumPy by full-batch gradient descent.\\
Intended use & Teaching demonstration of synthetic fan-condition failure-risk scoring and interpretable operator support.\\
Out-of-scope use & Real maintenance prediction, safety control, autonomous work orders, regulatory/contractual decisions, or probability claims about real assets.\\
Training data & 2,400 deterministic independent synthetic snapshots, seed 20260805; 1,920 train and 480 random holdout.\\
Features & filter-clog fraction, fan-speed fraction, vibration mm/s, bearing temperature \si{\celsius}, run hours.\\
Target & Synthetic Bernoulli label named \code{failure\_within\_7d}; no seven-day trajectory or observed failure.\\
Preprocessing & Train-only mean/standard-deviation scaling.\\
Training & 2,400 iterations, learning rate 0.08, no regularization.\\
Output & $p=\sigma(b+\sum\beta_jz_j)$ clipped at logit $\pm50$; low $<0.35$, medium $[0.35,0.65)$, high $\ge0.65$.\\
Stored evidence & At cutoff 0.5: accuracy 0.8042, precision 0.7480, recall 0.5935; TP 92, FP 31, FN 63, TN 294.\\
Explanation & Signed standardized log-odds terms; top three sorted by signed value. Associative, not causal.\\
Known limitations & Generator circularity; snapshot horizon; one random split; no grouped/temporal/external validation, confidence intervals, calibration, drift/OOD/missing-data policy, cost-based threshold, or subgroup analysis.\\
Human oversight & Recommendation only. Risk does not alter coordinator, fan speed, setpoint, airflow limit, or maintenance state.\\
Required validation & Executed notebook, realistic trajectories/assets, baselines, calibration, PR/ROC, interval estimates, threshold cost, OOD/missingness, prospective shadow trial.\\
Governance & Version, feature schema, means/scales, coefficients and thresholds are inspectable; production requires ownership, approval, signed artifact, monitoring and rollback.\\
