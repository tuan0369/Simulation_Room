\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
EcoHVAC Guardian extends a single-room teaching twin into a centrally coordinated ecosystem in which two independently controlled room twins contend for one finite-capacity air-handling unit (AHU). Each room forms an airflow request through a local PID loop. A transparent lexicographic coordinator assigns the shared supply according to occupancy and positive temperature error; the grant then updates room temperature and humidity, AHU energy, filter loading, fan condition, and an interpretable logistic failure-risk estimate. The implemented ecosystem therefore closes a deterministic sense--decide--coordinate--act--verify loop. Predictive intelligence is integrated at runtime but remains advisory: it neither changes airflow nor creates a work order.

This report derives the implemented thermal, moisture, control, allocation, degradation, energy, data-generation, training, and inference equations; records all principal units and bounds; and traces the HW2 requirements to source and evidence. At a classification threshold of 0.5, the checked-in synthetic holdout records accuracy 0.8042, balanced accuracy 0.7491, precision 0.7480, recall 0.5935, specificity 0.9046, F1 0.6619, ROC-AUC 0.8449, Brier score 0.1467, and log loss 0.4471 (92 true positives, 31 false positives, 63 false negatives, and 294 true negatives). These results show recovery of a seeded synthetic generator, not field validity: independent snapshots are labelled through a logistic relationship using the same predictors learned by the model, and the nominal seven-day target is not generated by seven-day trajectories. Live evidence demonstrates two-room resource contention and current governance/strategy interfaces. Security and ROI are explicitly bounded as, respectively, prototype controls plus a target architecture, and illustrative assumptions rather than measured outcomes.

The principal contribution is a working, explainable two-room multi-twin simulation with autonomous deterministic coordination and predictive-maintenance decision support. Five rooms are not claimed as implemented; they are analysed only as a future fairness and scalability test.
